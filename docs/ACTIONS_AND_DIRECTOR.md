# Describing a shot with actions, life and the director

_Iteration 4. Applies to scene files (`*.scene.json`). Everything here is compiled when the scene is loaded; keys
you set by hand in `"animation"` always override what actions and the director generate._

A complete example is `content/scenes/watertable_v4.scene.json` (generated by `tools/make_watertable_v4.py`):
nothing in it is keyed by hand.

## Life (per character)
```json
"characters": [{"name": "A", "asset": "../characters/girl_white_hair.character.json",
                "position": [0.38, 0.012, 0.15], "yawDeg": -25, "life": true}]
```
`"life": true` turns on breathing, slow weight shift, small head drift, automatic blinks (about every 3.6 s,
jittered) and the head following the gaze with a lag. Fine control: `"life": {"breathing": 1, "sway": 1,
"headDrift": 1, "blinkInterval": 3.6, "headFollow": 0.55, "headLagSec": 0.2}` (0 turns a part off).

## Actions
`"actions": [ ... ]`, each with `"type"`, `"character"` and frames. Order in the file does not matter.

| Type | Fields | What it generates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `look` | `start`, one of `object` / `target:[x,y,z]` / `camera:true` / `atCharacter` / `atProp` (T-112a: a prop's middle, followed as it moves), `end` (follow a moving object until) | Eye gaze with a quick 3-frame saccade; with life on, the head follows later |
| `expression` | `name` (smile, frown, surprise, mouthClose, …), `start`, `end`, `weight`, `ramp` | Eases the expression in and back out |
| `reach` | `hand` (L/R), `object`, `start`, `contact`, `lift`, `liftEnd`, `place`, `placeAt:[x,y,z]`, `release`, `end`, `graspRadius`, `graspAxis`, `look` | Hand opens on the way, arm IK to the object (leans the upper body if it is beyond arm's length), fingers close by contact search, object attached, lifted towards the body, put down, released, hand returns; gaze follows the object; ripples if the object floats on water |
| `touch` | `hand`, `start`, `contact`, `end`, `target:[x,y,z]` | Points and touches the target with the index fingertip (corrected to land within ~2 mm when in reach); ripples on water |
| `walk` | `start`, `end`, `to:[x,z]`, `face` (final heading, degrees) | Eased path, turn into the path, planted footsteps (foot IK), pelvis bob and lean, counter-swinging arms |

Warnings are printed when a reach/touch target stays out of reach even after leaning ("move them closer").

### Prop interactions (T-022, `engine/scene/interaction.{h,cpp}`)
These work on scene `props` (`plush.asset/1`, `docs/ASSET_LIBRARY.md`) and on any character: the hand is the shared
`hand.L`/`hand.R` role, the palm contact point and hand axes come from the finger roles and the skeleton's hand frame.

| Type | Fields | What it generates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `grasp` (or `reach` with `prop`) | `hand` (L/R), `prop`, `socket`="grip", `start`, `contact`=start+14, `grasp`=true, `end` (only with grasp:false), `look`=true | Arm IK so the palm point lands on the socket at `contact` (corrected to < 0.3 mm when in reach), palm turned towards the prop (thumb along a cylinder's axis, fingers away from the body), hand opens on the way and closes by contact search sized to the prop, the prop's parent switches to the hand at `contact` (rebased: no pop), gaze on the prop |
| `hold` | `hand`, `prop`?, `start`, `end`, `at:[x,y,z]` (prop origin) or `by:[dx,dy,dz]` | Moves the held prop, keeping its world orientation (a cup stays upright) |
| `place` | `hand`, `prop`?, `start`, `contact`=start+16, `end`=contact+14, `at:[x,y,z]` or `onto:{prop, socket}`, `socket`="place" | The prop's `socket` lands on the target at `contact`, where it detaches (or re-parents to `onto`); fingers open, the hand backs away, the arm returns by `end` |
| `handover` | `hand`, `to`, `toHand`="R", `prop`?, `toSocket`="grip_b", `start`, `receiverStart`=start+4, `contact`=start+18, `release`=contact+4, `end`=release+12, `at:[x,y,z]`? | The giver brings the prop to `at` (default between the two) and holds it still; the receiver reaches its own socket and grasps at `contact`, where the parent switches to the receiver; the giver lets go at `release`, looks at the receiver and withdraws by `end` |

A hand holds one prop at a time; `hold`/`place`/`handover` need the hand to hold it (load errors otherwise). Contacts
are re-checked after all actions are compiled; a contact that stays more than 5 mm off (out of reach) is reported in
the scene's warnings. Generated tracks: `<who>/ik.<hand bone>`, `<who>/orient.<hand bone>` (+ `.weight`; the hand's
world rotation, applied after IK), `<who>/hand.<side>` and its grasp tracks, `<who>/lookAt`.

**Arm-body clearance (T-030, `interaction.h`).** Every IK-driven arm is kept out of its own body:
- *Body model* (per character, from the shared roles and the skinned rest meshes, garments included): seven short
  sections along the spine (2 pelvis bands under the hips, 3 lower-torso bands hips → chest, 2 upper-torso bands
  chest → neck), each an elliptical capsule fitted to the 99th percentile of its own height slab (front half: hands
  work in front). The arm is its own skinned surface (sleeve, cuff, hand: about 600 sampled vertices with their real
  weights), not bone capsules, because a garment sleeve need not follow the arm bones the way skin does.
- *Measure:* the gap of the hand and the wrist half of the forearm to the sections (negative = inside). A point that
  already rests against the body in the rest pose counts from that depth. The upper arm and the elbow half of the
  forearm rest against the side naturally: they are reported, not required.
- *Elbow pole* (`<who>/pole.<hand bone>` + `.weight` = the IK weight): the upper arm swings about the shoulder → wrist
  axis so the elbow points out, down and a little back (chest frame); the wrist stays and the hand keeps its rotation.
- *Wrist target:* per frame, pushed along the gap gradient until the arm keeps 5 mm (`kArmBodyMargin`). Each frame starts
  from the previous frame's push and is shrunk to the smallest push that clears; the pushes are then eased in time (no
  pops). While the IK fades in or out the arm may be as deep as the clip arm alone is, never deeper. Contacts and the
  giver's still hold of a hand-over keep their exact targets; `reach`/`touch` contacts too.
- *Hand-over:* the meeting point (`at`, or the default) is searched on a grid around the requested point (3 cm, then
  1 cm steps) for the closest point that keeps both arms clear and within reach; the receiver's reach is planned there.
- *Reports:* an arm still inside at full IK weight (`'<who>' arm R passes N mm into the body at frame F`) and a hand-over
  without a clear meeting point are scene warnings. A contact the body is in the way of cannot be moved (the prop is
  where the author put it): stage props in front of the body.

### Verbs (T-112a, T-112b; `engine/scene/verbs.{h,cpp}`, `content/verbs/*.verb.json`)
A verb action is expanded **before** the compile into the primitive actions above (plus generated `look` actions),
with every frame filled from the verb's phases (design: `docs/architecture/INTERACTION_AFFORDANCES.md`). A scene
without a verb action is compiled exactly as before.

| Type | Fields | Lowers to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pick_up` | `hand`="R" or `hands`:["L","R"] (T-112b), `prop`, `set` / `site` (prop affordances) or `socket`, `start`?, `contact`?, `anticipate`?, `settle`?, `fingers`?, `look`=true | `grasp` (+ a follower `grasp` for the second hand) |
| `carry` | `hand` or `hands`, `prop`?, `start`?, `end`?, `at:[x,y,z]` or `by:[dx,dy,dz]`, `settle`?, `look` | `hold` |
| `place` with `"verb": true` | `hand` or `hands`, `prop`?, `start`?, `contact`?, `end`?, `at` or `onto:{prop, socket}`, `socket`?, `settle`?, `react`?, `look` | `place` |
| `pass` (T-112b) | `hand`="R", `to`, `toHand`="R", `prop`?, `toSet` / `toSite` / `toSocket`?, `start`?, `receiverStart`?, `contact`?, `release`?, `end`?, `at`?, `settle`?, `react`?, `look` | `handover` |

- **Chaining.** A verb without `start` begins where the previous verb of the same hand ended, settle included. After
  a `pass` the receiving hand's next verb begins at the giver's release.
- **Phases.** The phases are look, anticipate, approach, contact, manipulate, release and settle. Each takes its beat
  from the scene's timing profile (`"timing"`), times the character's personality tempo. Without a profile it takes
  today's default: reach 14, hold 16, place 16, release 14, and a gaze lead of 4.
- **Optional phases.** Anticipate and settle are emitted only with a profile, or when the action asks
  (`true`, or a number of frames). So without a profile, `pick_up` + `carry` + `place` expand to exactly the
  hand-written `grasp` + `hold` + `place` (tested key for key).
- **Look.** With a profile the look is a generated `look` at the prop (or at the place target). It ends where the
  anticipation starts, so the eyes lead the hand.
- **Grasp site.** `set` or `site` names a grasp site of the prop's `affordances` (`docs/ASSET_LIBRARY.md`). Without
  them, the first one-site set this hand may use is taken, else `socket` (default `grip`).
- **Hand shapes (T-112b, values from the T-051 table since T-112c).** A site's `shape` other than `wrap` goes into
  the grasp as `"shape"` (and its `approach` side as `"approach"`). `reachTo` looks the shape up in the hand-shape
  table (`content/hand_shapes/<shape>.hand_shape.json`, found above the scene; `engine/anim/hand_shape.h`). It keys
  the shape's `approach` pose on the way in and its `pose` at contact (`keyHandShape`: `hand.<s>`, `.index`, `.f<k>`,
  `.thumb` and `.stagger` where the shape sets them), and the release opens it again. The shape's `search` decides
  the contact search: `none` (palm, hook, cradle) rests the hand as posed; `pinch` closes index and thumb on a
  sphere of `pinch_radius` just inside the socket, with the other fingers keyed free. `wrap` is today's grasp,
  unchanged: the table's wrap is `kOpen` and the all-finger search. A name that is not a `grasp` shape of the table
  is an error.

  | shape | approach (curl, thumb, spread) | pose | search |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `palm` | 0.08, 0.3, 0 | 0.15, 0.35, 0 | none |
  | `hook` | 0.2, 0.1, 0 | 0.7, 0.1, 0 | none |
  | `cradle` | 0.1, 0.25, 0.2 | 0.3, 0.25, 0.2 | none |
  | `pinch` | 0.1, 0.2, 0.1, index 0.1 | 0.6, 0.2, 0.1, index 0.1 (T-112b's inline pose: thumb 0.5, index 0.35) | index + thumb, 1 cm sphere |
- **Box faces (T-112c).** On a box prop (a box primitive, or a box collision proxy) a site grasp also gets
  `"graspFace": true` under the finger gate (as the band). When the socket is on a box face, `wrap`, `pinch` and
  `palm` then key the per-finger face search of T-051: `.grasp` (mid-plane point), `.graspRadius` (half thickness),
  `.graspFace` (inward normal, hand rest frame, Step) and `.grasp.weight` (0 → 1 over the last 4 f, back to 0 on
  release). The fingertips rest on the face, none inside it (felt tray: within 0.02 mm, boy and girl test skeletons).
  `hook` and `cradle` stay as posed.
- **Finger band (T-112b).** A site's `fingers` block goes into the grasp as `"band"` + `"thumb"`. The grasp then
  keys `hand.<s>.band` = (lo, hi, thumb) as Step keys at the reach start. lo and hi are the band relative to the grasp
  centre along the grasp axis (metres); thumb is 0 opposed, 1 along, 2 tucked. T-051 reads the track; before T-051
  it changes nothing. The band is written when the scene has a timing profile or the action sets `"fingers": true`
  (never with `"fingers": false`). Without a profile the expansion stays the T-112a legacy one.
- **Two hands (T-112b).** Set `"hands": ["L", "R"]`; the first hand leads. `set` names a two-site grasp set, else the
  asset's first two-site set is used. Its sites are matched to the two hands by their `hand`. The leading hand's
  grasp makes it the prop's parent. The second hand is a follower grasp (`"follower": true`): it contacts
  clamp(round(reaction_delay / 3), 1, `simultaneous_frames`) frames after the leader. From its contact to the place
  contact, its wrist is keyed in the prop frame at every frame. The clearance pass holds that span still, and the
  palm is corrected onto its socket at every frame with the other contacts. `carry` and `place` act through the
  leading hand, and both hands let go at the place contact. A prop with `"hands": "two"` (the felt tray,
  `content/assets/felt_tray.asset.json`) needs `hands`. A two-site set without `hands` is an error.
- **Pass (T-112b).** The receiver's look, reach and contact, the shared hold and the giver's release are the
  `handover` frames. Without a profile they are today's handover defaults (receiverStart +4, contact +18, release +4,
  end +12), tested key for key. With a profile, the look and reach beats are the receiver's (`reaction_delay`,
  `reach`) and the shared hold and release beats are the giver's. The receiver's socket is `toSocket`, else
  `toSite` / `toSet`, else the asset's `handover.receive` set, else `grip_b`.
- **Reactions (T-112b).** `"react": true` on `pass` / `place` adds an event when the scene has `"events"`. For a
  pass, the event is `partner_acts` (source the giver, at the pass start, for the receiver). For a place, it is
  `success` (at the settle start). The event is planned with the scene's own events (T-097D). A reacting pass has a
  single gaze source:
  - the receiver's reach starts at the reaction's notice (`planReaction`: reaction_delay x tempo, never under the
    personality's `delay_frames` low end) + 4 f, rounded up to a whole frame;
  - the handover plays no gaze of its own;
  - the giver's gaze is generated as `look` actions (at the prop until the release, then at the receiver);
  - the reaction's look (at the giver) is the receiver's notice, and from the reach start a generated `look` puts
    the receiver's eyes on the prop until contact + 8.
- **Failures are load errors,** formatted `verb '<verb>': character '<who>', prop '<prop>': <code>: <reason>`.
  - Checked while expanding:
    - `hand_full` and `hand_empty`;
    - `holds_other`;
    - `prop_held` (names the holder);
    - `unknown_site` and `unknown_set`;
    - `wrong_hand`;
    - `two_hands`;
    - `deform_unsupported`;
    - `affordances_invalid`;
    - `target_occupied` (another prop already sits on that socket);
    - `onto_self`;
    - `unknown_target`;
    - `no_start`;
    - T-112b: `same_hand` (the receiver is the giving hand);
    - `hands_meet` (the same socket, or finger bands that overlap on the same side of the prop);
    - `shared_too_short` (shared hold under 6 f, for authored or profile frames; the no-profile default of 4 f is
      today's handover);
    - `bad_hand`;
    - a two-hand prop given one hand, a two-hand pass, or a carry with `hands` of a prop held in one (`two_hands`).
  - Checked on the compiled motion (`checkVerbConstraints`):
    - `out_of_reach` (the wrist target is more than 5 mm past 0.98 x arm length; the error gives the shortfall);
    - `contact_error` (palm more than 0.5 mm off its socket at contact, or the place socket more than 0.5 mm off its
      target);
    - `upright_exceeded` (the prop's `upright` axis tilts past `max_tilt_deg` in a carry or a lower);
    - T-112b: `out_of_reach` for the pass receiver and giver, and for the second hand of a two-hand carry or place;
    - `arm_in_body`: an arm of a two-hand grasp, or of a pass at its meeting point, inside the body at full IK
      weight. This check needs the compile's `armGap` binding (VerbGeometry). Without the binding it is skipped,
      and the compile's own warning remains.

  The same reach written as primitives stays a warning.
- **Not yet:** the surface-normal constraint, a two-hand `pass`, the pass receiver's site shape (the handover receiver
  grasps as `wrap`), the
  `under` cradle site as a one-hand set, and verbs for generated (reaction) actions.

## Director (automatic camera)
```json
"director": {"minShot": 36, "closeFocal": 70, "closeFStop": 2.8, "mediumFocal": 50, "mediumFStop": 4}
```
The scene's `camera` is the master (wide) shot. Shots are planned from the actions:

| Beat | Shot |
| --- | --- |
| reach starts | medium on the character and the object |
| contact | insert close-up on the hand and object (follows it while it lifts) |
| holding | close-up of the face together with the object |
| placing | insert close-up on the place point |
| touch | insert close-up on the fingertip and the water |
| expression (when nothing else is on screen) | close-up of the face |
| otherwise | master wide |

Rules: shots shorter than ~0.7 × `minShot` are dropped; overlaps are cut in favour of the later action; gaps shorter
than `minShot` are absorbed by the previous shot; cameras stay on the master camera's side, rotate up to ±75° to
clear other characters, focus on the subject and push in by 8% over the shot. The shot list is printed when the
scene loads (`director: 53.0-77.0 insert: grip (70 mm f/2.8, 0.69 m)`).

To override: key `camera.position`, `camera.target` or `camera.lens` by hand — those tracks then win.

## Not implemented (do not rely on)
- Collision between bodies and furniture (an arm can pass over or through a table rim).
- Natural-language or external-assistant control: a JSON action list is the intended target for such a layer,
  but no integration exists yet.
- Running, sitting, turning on the spot without walking, two-handed grasps. (Handing a prop between characters:
  `handover`, T-022.) Prop interactions do not lean the body: keep the prop within arm's length, and in front of the body (T-030).
