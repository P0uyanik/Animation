# Describing a shot with actions, life and the director

_Iteration 4. Applies to scene files (`*.scene.json`). Everything here is compiled when the scene is loaded; keys
you set by hand in `"animation"` always override what actions and the director generate._

A complete example is `content/scenes/watertable_v4.scene.json` (generated by `tools/make_watertable_v4.py`):
nothing in it is keyed by hand.

## Life (per character)
```json
"characters": [{"name": "A", "asset": "../characters/girl_white_hair.character.json",
                "position": [0.38, 0.012, 0.15], "yawDeg": -25, "life": true}]
```
`"life": true` turns on breathing, slow weight shift, small head drift, automatic blinks (about every 3.6 s,
jittered) and the head following the gaze with a lag. Fine control: `"life": {"breathing": 1, "sway": 1,
"headDrift": 1, "blinkInterval": 3.6, "headFollow": 0.55, "headLagSec": 0.2}` (0 turns a part off).

## Actions
`"actions": [ ... ]`, each with `"type"`, `"character"` and frames. Order in the file does not matter.

| Type | Fields | What it generates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `look` | `start`, one of `object` / `target:[x,y,z]` / `camera:true`, `end` (follow a moving object until) | Eye gaze with a quick 3-frame saccade; with life on, the head follows later |
| `expression` | `name` (smile, frown, surprise, mouthClose, …), `start`, `end`, `weight`, `ramp` | Eases the expression in and back out |
| `reach` | `hand` (L/R), `object`, `start`, `contact`, `lift`, `liftEnd`, `place`, `placeAt:[x,y,z]`, `release`, `end`, `graspRadius`, `graspAxis`, `look` | Hand opens on the way, arm IK to the object (leans the upper body if it is beyond arm's length), fingers close by contact search, object attached, lifted towards the body, put down, released, hand returns; gaze follows the object; ripples if the object floats on water |
| `touch` | `hand`, `start`, `contact`, `end`, `target:[x,y,z]` | Points and touches the target with the index fingertip (corrected to land within ~2 mm when in reach); ripples on water |
| `walk` | `start`, `end`, `to:[x,z]`, `face` (final heading, degrees) | Eased path, turn into the path, planted footsteps (foot IK), pelvis bob and lean, counter-swinging arms |

Warnings are printed when a reach/touch target stays out of reach even after leaning ("move them closer").

### Prop interactions (T-022, `engine/scene/interaction.{h,cpp}`)
These work on scene `props` (`plush.asset/1`, `docs/ASSET_LIBRARY.md`) and on any character: the hand is the shared
`hand.L`/`hand.R` role, the palm contact point and hand axes come from the finger roles and the skeleton's hand frame.

| Type | Fields | What it generates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `grasp` (or `reach` with `prop`) | `hand` (L/R), `prop`, `socket`="grip", `start`, `contact`=start+14, `grasp`=true, `end` (only with grasp:false), `look`=true | Arm IK so the palm point lands on the socket at `contact` (corrected to < 0.3 mm when in reach), palm turned towards the prop (thumb along a cylinder's axis, fingers away from the body), hand opens on the way and closes by contact search sized to the prop, the prop's parent switches to the hand at `contact` (rebased: no pop), gaze on the prop |
| `hold` | `hand`, `prop`?, `start`, `end`, `at:[x,y,z]` (prop origin) or `by:[dx,dy,dz]` | Moves the held prop, keeping its world orientation (a cup stays upright) |
| `place` | `hand`, `prop`?, `start`, `contact`=start+16, `end`=contact+14, `at:[x,y,z]` or `onto:{prop, socket}`, `socket`="place" | The prop's `socket` lands on the target at `contact`, where it detaches (or re-parents to `onto`); fingers open, the hand backs away, the arm returns by `end` |
| `handover` | `hand`, `to`, `toHand`="R", `prop`?, `toSocket`="grip_b", `start`, `receiverStart`=start+4, `contact`=start+18, `release`=contact+4, `end`=release+12, `at:[x,y,z]`? | The giver brings the prop to `at` (default between the two) and holds it still; the receiver reaches its own socket and grasps at `contact`, where the parent switches to the receiver; the giver lets go at `release`, looks at the receiver and withdraws by `end` |

A hand holds one prop at a time; `hold`/`place`/`handover` need the hand to hold it (load errors otherwise). Contacts
are re-checked after all actions are compiled; a contact that stays more than 5 mm off (out of reach) is reported in
the scene's warnings. Generated tracks: `<who>/ik.<hand bone>`, `<who>/orient.<hand bone>` (+ `.weight`; the hand's
world rotation, applied after IK), `<who>/hand.<side>` and its grasp tracks, `<who>/lookAt`.

## Director (automatic camera)
```json
"director": {"minShot": 36, "closeFocal": 70, "closeFStop": 2.8, "mediumFocal": 50, "mediumFStop": 4}
```
The scene's `camera` is the master (wide) shot. Shots are planned from the actions:

| Beat | Shot |
| --- | --- |
| reach starts | medium on the character and the object |
| contact | insert close-up on the hand and object (follows it while it lifts) |
| holding | close-up of the face together with the object |
| placing | insert close-up on the place point |
| touch | insert close-up on the fingertip and the water |
| expression (when nothing else is on screen) | close-up of the face |
| otherwise | master wide |

Rules: shots shorter than ~0.7 × `minShot` are dropped; overlaps are cut in favour of the later action; gaps shorter
than `minShot` are absorbed by the previous shot; cameras stay on the master camera's side, rotate up to ±75° to
clear other characters, focus on the subject and push in by 8% over the shot. The shot list is printed when the
scene loads (`director: 53.0-77.0 insert: grip (70 mm f/2.8, 0.69 m)`).

To override: key `camera.position`, `camera.target` or `camera.lens` by hand — those tracks then win.

## Not implemented (do not rely on)
- Collision between bodies and furniture (an arm can pass over or through a table rim).
- Natural-language or external-assistant control: a JSON action list is the intended target for such a layer,
  but no integration exists yet.
- Running, sitting, turning on the spot without walking, two-handed grasps. (Handing a prop between characters:
  `handover`, T-022.) Prop interactions do not lean the body: keep the prop within arm's length.
