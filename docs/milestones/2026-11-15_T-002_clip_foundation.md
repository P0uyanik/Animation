## Implemented and tested (numbers: `docs/evaluation/animation/T-002/README.md`)
- **C1, one clip for every humanoid:** each clip file plays on the boy, the girl and the mannequin.
  - There is no per-character code, data file or clip copy.
  - Retarget data is generated by the exporter, or derived from the skeleton.
- **C2, correct retargeting:** boy vs girl, world direction and twist of the driven limb, torso and head roles, every
  clip frame, life off.
  - Whole-body clips: 0.0000°.
  - Arm-only clips: ≤ 0.95° (each arm is local to its own character's rest chest). Limits 2.0° / 5.0°.
  - Synthetic skeletons with different rest poses and proportions: 0.00003° (limit 0.5°).
- **C3, faithful transfer:** the engine playing each exported clip on the mannequin's package vs the Blender
  mannequin pose: ≤ 0.047° direction, ≤ 0.043° twist (limits 1.0° / 3.0°).
- **C4, smooth blending:**
  - Interior maxima are reported per clip and role.
  - All 22 blend-in/out and layering boundaries (both characters) are within min(12°, 1.25 × the larger neighbouring
    interior maximum); the root is within 1.25 × the interior root speed.
  - Loop seam: 0.22 × the interior maximum; root continuous.
  - Entry and exit frames equal the underlying pose (≤ 2e-6°).
- **C5, layering** (unit tests):
  - an arm clip during a walk changes only its masked arm; the other arm keeps the walk swing; legs, hips and root
    are unchanged (owner-approved rule (a));
  - walk keeps legs, hips and root under a full-body clip;
  - reach IK reaches its target with an arm clip on the same arm (< 1 mm);
  - hand keys win;
  - look is applied after the clips;
  - the e901 shot combines walk, wave and look, and point with a reach.
- **C6, unchanged without clips:**

  - (ii) Branch production run of s010 vs (i): only the two package-path fields differ (approved decision (b)).
  - 0 differing pixels for e900 frames 20–22 and for s010 frames 5–7 with the same girl master (see the note in
    Limits).
  - `ctest` 4/4 (frozen images unchanged).
- **C7, production path:** the e901 shot through `run_shot.py` (full bake plus contact-sheet frames) and Studio's

## Not verified
- **Acting quality:** out of scope. The clips are mechanical script poses, judged only for technical plausibility at
  320×180.
- **Fine contact at high resolution:** hands on thighs, the seat, foot planting.
- **A real Blender 5.3 binary:** only the bpy 5.3 module ran.
- **Third and further characters:** C2 is shown on two characters plus the mannequin and synthetic skeletons.

## Decisions and assumptions (approved by the Supervisor where marked)
- **(a) approved:** an arm clip replaces the walk's arm swing only on its mask; walk keeps legs, hips and root.
- **(b) approved:** C6 is proven on two levels, because the required cache-key change renames package folders.
- **Reference pose:** a T-pose (mocap-friendly). Semantic parents are used because arms, thighs and eyes are
  top-level bones in the Rigify packages.
- **Clips carry their reference pose:** each clip embeds a copy of the profile's reference-pose block, so a clip is
  self-describing. The profile remains the single definition.
- **Masks:** a clip drives only the roles it animates (sit and stand leave arms, hips and head to the base layer).
  Non-looping clips hold their first and last poses for about 8 frames, so blends happen on near-static poses. This
  is needed for the 1.25× limits against a breathing base idle.
- **Walk interior maximum for C4:** taken from the unchanged engine walk over its whole span. Its first step is
  faster than mid-walk; that is existing behaviour.
- **Finger tracks:** applied after the hand-pose system unless a hand track (reach, touch, hand keys) owns the hand.

## Limits
- **Rigid shoulders and hips:** arm and thigh pivots of the Rigify packages do not follow the chest or hips (top-level
  bones). Clips keep torso bends small.
- **Foot sliding:** root motion scales with the leg length, so characters whose thigh/leg ratio differs from the
  mannequin's can slide their feet slightly while sitting. Foot-plant contacts are recorded for a later IK fix-up.
- **Box props:** static in bake v1 (a reached cup does not move in the Blender render).
- **Girl master rebuild:** rebuilding the unpublished girl's master from her spec is not byte-identical (pre-existing).
  A fresh rebuild changed up to 54 pixels by 1/255 in s010.
