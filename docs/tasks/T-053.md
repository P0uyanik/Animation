## Acceptance criteria
1. **One profile reader** (`engine/anim/personality.{h,cpp}`, registered in CMake with a stub) resolves
   `content/personality/<id>.json` (`plush.personality/0`, `content/personality/SCHEMA.md`) once into typed parameters;
   every layer receives them (life layer: blink/breath/weight shift; face layer: rest and expression bias; clip
   layers: tempo; gaze; body mechanics posture — replace its own reader in `engine/anim/body_mechanics.cpp`).
2. Profiles derived from the references and owner decisions only (`docs/characters/*/REFERENCE_BEHAVIOUR.md`,
   `docs/process/OWNER_DECISIONS.md`): **boy** subtle, calm, low-energy (heavy lids, long stares, slow small moves,
   never abrupt); **girl** cheerful and intelligent (brown eyes, closed gentle smile at rest, open smiles on success,
   brisk reaches 8–10 f, hand-to-chin, pointing); **third** subtly goofy, bubbly, closed soft smile at rest, parted
   lips while watching, clasped hands. Promote the `.draft.json` files to versioned profiles.
3. A **`limits`** section per profile, clamped by the engine (with a warning) and checked by a personality check in
   `tools/animcheck/` (blink interval/duration, reach frames, hold lengths, motion amplitude vs profile ranges).
4. Profile `false`/absent keeps today's behaviour bit for bit (regression shots unchanged).
5. Evidence: per character one idle + one gesture clip, before | after (≤ 6 Cycles renders total, the rest Workbench
   or engine previews), plus the animcheck personality report. Tests in `tests/test_personality.cpp`.
6. Gate full tier passes except guarded paths (list them).
