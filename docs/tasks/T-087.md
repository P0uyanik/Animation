# T-087: comparison recipe lock and fidelity fixes (Phase A, round 2)

Implementer: Claude (cloud) · lane E · path A · branch `t-087-claude-review-recipe` from `origin/main`.
Round 2 plan A3. Owner-approved 2026-11-18.

## Why (audit evidence)
- 10 of 26 boy 2.0.0 evidence stills were rendered on A-pose masters and were unusable.
- 11 showcase frames were discarded after the cast changed.
- `tools/fidelity/run.py` can silently fall back to an A-pose master, and its hidden-object filter hides the backpack.
- The 2-point fidelity drop blocked the girl although the owner judged her clearly better.
- Owner sheets differ per task. The T-081 strict sheet (identical cameras and lights, rendered in one run) let the
  owner decide in minutes.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Recipe file and tool** (`tools/review/recipe.py`): a visual task declares in one JSON:
   - the input SHAs;
   - the published baseline version;
   - the candidate (spec, overrides, SHA);
   - the compare variant (pose and master);
   - cameras (reference yaws), light rig, samples and resolution.

   The tool validates it, refuses A-pose or wrong-variant inputs unless explicitly requested, and records it next to
   the outputs.
2. **First-frame check:** `recipe.py preview` renders **one cheap frame** (Workbench or low-sample) of every view.
   Long renders refuse to start without a recorded preview (an override flag exists and is logged).
3. **Standard strict sheet** (`recipe.py sheet`):
   - columns: original reference | previous approved | candidate (optional: Higgsfield target, labelled "AI target");
   - rows: front, 3/4, side, back, face front, face 3/4;
   - identical cameras and lights, rendered in one run;
   - the layout follows `docs/evaluation/characters/T-081/girl_0.10.0_vs_0.11.0_strict.jpg`.
4. **Fidelity fixes:**
   - remove the silent A-pose fallback in `tools/fidelity/run.py` (fail with a clear message instead);
   - fix the hidden-object filter so visible garments (backpack) are measured.
5. **Rule change:** a fidelity drop above 2 points **flags "needs owner review"** (gate PASS with a warning and a
   summary line) instead of failing. Regression image checks stay unchanged.
6. Tests for 1, 2, 4 and 5. Document the procedure in `docs/process/REVIEW_RECIPE.md` (one page) and add one line to
   `docs/process/IMPLEMENTER_PROMPT.md`.
