Track: Personality. Small (well under a day).

## Why
T-097A (emotion blending) and T-097B (reaction planner) both merged, but nothing schedules a `ReactionPlan` as an action:
reactions are computed and never played. T-097C's animcheck already reads an optional scene `events` key to measure
notice delays. T-097-boy and T-097-girl need the full chain.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Optional scene key `events` (same shape T-097C's `tools/animcheck/acting.py` reads; document it in the scene
   docs): each event becomes a `ReactionEvent`; for every opted-in actor the T-097B planner yields a `ReactionPlan`;
   playback schedules it as a blended emotion action (T-097A `blend`) after the notice delay, plus gaze habit and the
   chosen gesture.
2. Opt-in per scene; scenes without `events` bake byte-identical (show hashes for the animcheck shots, e902-e907 where
   possible, and the content scenes).
3. Tests: a small fixture scene where an event triggers a delayed, blended reaction; the T-097C acting check sees the
   delay; ordering is deterministic.
4. No per-character tuning (T-097-boy/girl). No test loosened. READY ends with the time-ledger line.
