## Content
One shot of ~8 s at 24 fps (192 frames): boy and girl at a table with the cup. It builds on e907 `s010_verbs` and the
T-114 acting scene. It must show:
- both characters' facial expressions;
- eye movements: eyes lead the head, glances at the cup and the partner, blinks;
- personality-driven reactions;
- gestures;
- object interaction: pick_up, carry, pass with a shared hold and release, and finger grasps.

Suggested beats:
| Time | Beats |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Cup on the table. Boy slouched and bored, with fidgets. Girl with her hand at her chin. |
| 2–4 s | The boy notices after his own delay, reaches, picks up and carries the cup. |
| 4–6 s | The girl reacts with wonder. Pass, shared hold, release. |
| 6–8 s | The girl is delighted (SmileOpen). The boy does a reluctant shrug or head lift and looks away. |
