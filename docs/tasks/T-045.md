# T-045: body mechanics (shoulders, clavicles, torso, weight shift)
Lane C · path A for engine/system code (visible motion improvement reviewed against the references) · branch `t-045-claude-body-mechanics` from `origin/staging`

## Why
Owner feedback: bodies look robotic, little shoulder and torso motion. References are the primary target:
`docs/characters/*/REFERENCE_BEHAVIOUR.md`, `docs/characters/REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md`, `content/personality/*.draft.json`
(boy: slouch, low energy, heavy settle; girl: upright, brisk reaches; third character: onlooker, clasped hands).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Shared, data-driven body mechanics in the engine (no character names in code):
   - clavicle/shoulder participation in arm motion (raise, reach, wave, point): shoulder shrug and protraction
     proportional to arm elevation/forward reach, with limits per rig profile;
   - torso/spine follow: chest and spine counter-rotate and lean into reaches and gestures; head stabilisation;
   - weight shift: pelvis shift + knee bend over the supporting leg during idle, reaches and gestures;
   - overlap/follow-through on the torso and arms (small delays and settle), tunable per character profile
     (tempo, energy, settle frames) via the personality draft fields; defaults neutral.
2. Characters whose rig lacks clavicles or spine segments degrade gracefully (skip, no warnings spam).
3. Existing clips keep their intent: all clip, interaction and emotion tests pass unchanged (no expectation changes);
   hand contacts in interaction shots still ≤ 5 mm (do not edit interaction code — T-040r1 owns it).
4. Evidence: one 4–6 s render (Cycles low samples + OIDN, ≤ 2 renders) of both characters doing idle → reach → wave,
   before/after side by side, compared against the reference poses; curves of clavicle/spine/pelvis channels.
5. Full gate PASS.
