# T-040r1: arm-body clearance from the real mesh (redesign of T-040)

## Why a redesign
T-040 (docs/evaluation/animation/T-040/README.md) fitted capsules/ellipses: they are fuller than the mesh (3 clip
tests over-corrected) or thinner (−1 mm overlaps), and deep corrections step 10–12°/frame.

## Acceptance criteria
1. At character load the engine builds a per-torso-bone clearance profile from the package's own skinned mesh
   (e.g. angular radius profile or coarse SDF in bone space; generic, no character names), used by the post-pass
   only; the interaction solver's `sectionGap` is unchanged.
2. Mesh distance ≥ 0 mm for both arms of both characters in e904, e906 and in e905 except while a wrist is pinned
   by a hold/hand-over (that case is T-040r2: report it, do not fake it).
3. Correction smooth: ≤ 2°/frame step and bounded acceleration everywhere (ease in/out over frames).
4. The 3 failing `test_clips` cases pass **without changing their expectations** (the poses are clear on the mesh).
5. plush_tests and CTest fully PASS; interaction contacts ≤ 5 mm; no prop jumps; interaction compile time not worse
   than T-040's (target: the 150 s doctest ≤ 60 s).
6. Evidence: mesh measurement JSON for e904/e905/e906 and an e906 contact sheet (no new video needed).
