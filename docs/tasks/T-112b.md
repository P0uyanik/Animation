## Why (evidence)
- The hand-over is authored as one primitive with hand-picked frames per shot (`make_shot.py`: `receiverStart`,
  `contact`, `release`, `end`); the receiver's notice is a separate `look` action, not the T-097 reaction system.
- Every grasp is a full-hand wrap: no palm, cradle, pinch or hook; no object needs two hands (PB S2/S4: the girl's
  careful two-handed handwork, `reference_timing.json` `pb_s2_boat_hold`, `pb_s4_lower`).

## Acceptance criteria
1. `content/verbs/pass.verb.json` and its expansion to `handover` (design 4.4): giver offer, receiver look /
   approach / contact, shared hold, giver release, settle; fallbacks = today's handover defaults (design 3.3), so a
   `pass` without profile equals the hand-written `handover` key for key (test).
2. `"react": true` on a verb appends `partner_acts` (pass) / `success` (place) events when the scene has `events`;
   the receiver's look then comes from the reaction (one gaze source). Test: notice delay = `reaction_delay` x tempo,
   boy and girl profiles.
3. Hand shapes `palm`, `cradle`, `pinch`, `hook` (design 2.3) in `reachTo` / `place` release, used only when the
   action carries a non-`wrap` shape; `wrap` = today's tracks (golden unchanged).
4. Two-handed grasp sets (design 2.4): leading hand owns the parent, follower wrist keyed in the prop frame,
   contacts within `simultaneous_frames`, both arms outside the bodies (`armClearancePass` residual ≥ 0), follower
   contacts corrected by `finishInteractions`. New asset `content/assets/felt_tray.asset.json` (design 2.6).
   Tests on boy and girl skeletons: pick up, carry, place of the tray; tilt ≤ 8°.
5. `hand.<s>.band` keys from a site's `fingers` block (design 5 contract), Step keys at the reach start.
6. Failure cases of design 4.4 and the two-hand cases, one test each.
7. `episodes/e907_exchange/shots/s010_verbs/` becomes the full exchange as verbs (current approved versions); one
   cheap preview, then one T-087 strict sheet compared with `s010_calibrated` (same timing expected; any visible
   difference explained). animcheck: no new warnings.
8. The T-112a golden test passes unchanged. No test loosened, no frozen or published file changed.
