## Questions to answer, with evidence
1. How close are the three characters to the original references, area by area?
   - Areas: face and expressions, eyes, hair, skin, materials, garments, proportions, animation, interactions.
   - Give a score and evidence pairs (reference file and frame → our file and frame) for each.
2. What are the most significant remaining visual and technical gaps, and their root causes in the code (file level)?
3. What concrete changes would bring us closest to the target quality, ordered by visual gain per effort?
4. How efficient is the development workflow?
   - Task assignment and task size, parallel execution, branch ownership and claims, conflict prevention, the
     merge and CI process, and agent communication (relay, messages).
   - Measure it: rework rounds per task, time to merge, CI minutes, superseded or unmerged branches, repeated work.
5. Are tokens or effort being wasted? Look for repeated work, oversized tasks, unnecessary process or complexity.
6. **Tools installed yesterday:** for each one, is it actually used, by what, how often, and with what measurable
   benefit? Which are unused?
7. Are we missing valuable open-source tools, libraries or techniques?
   - Areas: character modelling, hair and groom, materials and shading, facial rigging and expressions, animation,
     physics or cloth, character–object interaction.
   - Recommend only what has clear evidence of benefit. Avoid duplicate functionality and new dependencies without
     need.
8. What practical improvements would speed up development without losing quality, stability or reference fidelity?
