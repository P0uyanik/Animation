## Why
T-116: the T-051 finger grasps animate, but the fingers are fused tubes, so individual fingertips do not read when
grasping the cup.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Opt-in hand mesh (new module `pipeline/charsys/hands_r2.py`; `body_v6.py` untouched): separated fingers with a
   readable gap and rounded tips at the plush scale (#7, #24, #27); same bone names and skinning so T-051 grasps work.
2. Fingertips read in a close-up of the T-051 grasp (current vs candidate) and in the T-117 pickup camera.
3. Plain tests in `pipeline/charsys/tests/test_hands_r2.py` (bones, vertex groups, no intersecting fingers at rest and
   at full grasp); default builds identical.
