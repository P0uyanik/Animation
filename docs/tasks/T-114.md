## Acceptance criteria
1. A new episode, `episodes/e909_two_char_acting/`, about 8–12 s. The boy and the girl interact over a felt prop:
   - she notices it;
   - she picks it up and offers it;
   - he reacts reluctantly, then accepts;
   - he passes it back or places it;
   - both react.

   Built from:
   - scene `events` (T-097D reactions);
   - verbs (T-112) with finger grasps;
   - the per-character expressions (T-113);
   - the calibrated timing default (no `timing` key).

   Beat timings are cited against the reference videos (T-106 table).
2. The cast is `"current"`. Where a T-109/T-113 candidate option looks better, render it as a separate candidate-cast
   copy, and say which.
3. The checks pass on the bake:
   - acting.py, personality.py, timing.py and animcheck --check;
   - contact and band measures (T-051) on both hands;
   - no engine warnings.
4. Evidence:
   - a cheap preview first;
   - a strip against the reference frames;
   - a short Cycles mp4 (640x360) at final settings.
5. A test pins the shot's key beats (notice delay, handover frames, grasp contacts). Golden hashes stay unchanged for
   other scenes. No test loosened.
