## Why (evidence)
- `applyHandPose` (`engine/anim/animation.cpp`) closes all fingers with one curl search on one sphere or cylinder:
  no thumb opposition, no contact band, no per-finger follow-through; the giver's and receiver's fingers on the e907
  cup can only be kept apart by socket height.
- Roadmap row 2 (T-051): per-finger curl / spread / thumb opposition, hand-pose library, grasp shapes per prop size,
  finger follow-through, relaxed idle, gesture fingers (point, count, chin-touch; girl references).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Hand-shape table `content/hand_shapes/*.json` (`plush.hand_shape/1`): the five grasp shapes of design 2.3 (values
   there as the starting point), `relaxed`, `point`, `count_1..3`, `chin_touch`; loader + validation; no character
   names (per-character bias only through the personality profile).
2. Per-finger grasp in `applyHandPose` when a `hand.<s>.band` track exists (design 5 contract): each finger's contact
   clamped into the band, curl searched to the surface plus the fingertip radius, thumb opposed / along / tucked.
   Without the track: today's search, byte-identical (the T-112a golden test passes unchanged).
3. Optional per-finger tracks `hand.<s>.f<k>` (curl, spread) and `hand.<s>.thumb`; finger stagger 1 f index → little
   on close, reverse on open, only when the band track or a shape asks for it.
4. Measured on boy, girl and the engine skeleton, felt cup (both bands) and a box face: every fingertip within 2 mm
   of the surface, none inside the collision proxy, thumb on the opposite half; wrist and palm contact unchanged
   (≤ 0.01 mm against the same shot without the band).
5. Visual: one cheap preview of both hands on the cup at the e907 shared-hold frame, then one T-087 strict sheet
   (hands close-up, current approved versions) against the references (`docs/characters/*/REFERENCE_BEHAVIOUR.md`).
6. No test loosened; no frozen or published file changed; image regressions unchanged.
