# Owner test videos: the visual and animation benchmark (2026-11-15)

These two videos define the target for the look, motion, atmosphere and editing of finished Plush Studio episodes.
Every visual result is judged against them and the character reference sheets, never against descriptions.

| File | Length | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `video_watertable.mp4` | 5 s, 828×1108, 24 fps | All three characters at a wooden water table in a sunny kindergarten classroom, watching a paper boat. The girl talks and gestures, the boy leans in, the water ripples |
| `video_paperboat.mp4` | 12 s, 854×480, 24 fps | Several shots: the three characters fold paper at a classroom desk (close-ups of the hands folding a paper boat); the boat is placed on a water tub; ripples and golden sparkles appear; the characters react in wonder |

**What they show:**
- **Characters:** felted plush figures with wool hair and visible fibres, soft fabric clothing, large expressive eyes.
- **Environment and light:** a fully dressed classroom set with props (desks, crayons, paper, water tub), warm daylight, shallow depth of field.
- **Editing:** multi-shot editing with close-ups.
- **Acting:** expressive face and body acting, and fine hand work (folding and placing paper).
- **Effects:** water interaction and a small magical light effect.
