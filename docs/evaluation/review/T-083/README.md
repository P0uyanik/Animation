# T-083 · How close are the renders to the owner's references?

**Overall visual match: 4/10, or about six points short of the target.** At thumbnail size the heavy-lidded boy, the girl with glasses and the white-haired child are recognizable. At shot size the difference is what holds the three together. In the reference, all three look at a small bright boat, their fingers meet the rim, and daylight picks out the wool around their heads. In our water-table opening the boy is cut by the left edge, the boat is hard to find and the hands have little contrast against the table. In the newer acting test the faces read more clearly, but the shared object and classroom are gone. Compare WT f000, v4 f000 and T-046 f000 in the [staging](staging.jpg) and [acting](acting.jpg) sheets.

These are visual estimates for the best **locally inspectable evidence in each area**. The overall estimate judges a finished shot. It is not an average of diagnostic clips or a conversion of the character score. On this scale 0 means no recognizable correspondence, 5 means recognizable with conspicuous differences, and 10 means indistinguishable at the reference framing. The latest complete boy and girl builds have **reported** scores, but their full-build frames are unavailable locally. Confidence is lower for those two version judgements than for comparisons of the displayed frames.
