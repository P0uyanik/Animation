## 5. Verified vs. assumed
- **Verified:**
  - Camera conversion: Blender and Plush renders of the same camera line up.
  - Silhouettes: masks checked by eye (`references/views/*_check.png`).
  - The full run completes headless.
- **Assumed:**
  - Elevation and lens per view are estimates from visual cues, ±5° and ±20 mm. They bias IoU slightly but act
    equally on every subject, so comparisons between candidates stay fair.
- **Limits:**
  - Silhouettes ignore face and colour.
  - #27's back views show no backpack while the others do.
  - The OBJ stand-in has no procedural textures, so the eyes are white in the Blender renders.
