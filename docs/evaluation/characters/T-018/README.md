## What changed (production fix instead of per-pose offsets)

1. **Body masks.** Each shell garment records the body faces it covers
   (`ps_cover.<item>`). After rigging, a `MASK` modifier hides the faces that are
   fully covered by the worn items, minus a 15 mm geodesic margin
   (`mask_margin_m`) so skin stays under hems, cuffs and collars. Spec:
   `clothing.hide_body_faces`, per item `mask_body`.
2. **Post-skinning collision.** Each garment piece has, after its `Armature`
   modifier, 3 `SHRINKWRAP` passes (nearest surface point, `OUTSIDE`, 2 mm
   offset). They are limited to the vertex group `ps_collision`, which weights
   vertices over or within 80 mm of visible skin. The target is a hidden
   collider that shares the body mesh and weights but has no mask. Spec:
   `clothing.collision {enabled, offset_m, radius_m, falloff_m, passes}`.
3. **Clearance test.** Signed distance from every garment vertex to the posed,
   unmasked body. Only vertices whose nearest body point is on a visible
   (unmasked) face count. The checks are rest pose plus six clip poses from
   `content/clips`.
   - Rest must clear by at least 0.5 mm.
   - Posed, no vertex may be inside visible skin.
   - In posed creases the nearest-face sign is unreliable, so a vertex counts as
     inside only if a ray-parity test against the closed body agrees.
   - Vertices pressed against skin that is itself in skin-to-skin contact are
     reported separately as `skin_contact`, not counted. That is skin pushed
     inside another body part, or skin facing another part closer than
     2 x offset + 0.5 mm.

The articulation offsets Codex tuned by hand (allowance, front rise, front pleat)
are still available as spec fields but now default to 0.

Before this change (Codex snapshot `8579c64`, whole-body metric, no masks):
`sit_down:50` gave outfit -21.3 mm (shorts) and dress -2.9 mm. With the same
whole-body metric and masks plus collision, but before the crease-robust
inside test, the residuals were -1.4 mm (arm on ribs, idle) and about -1 mm
(sit). Those are now classified as skin contact. The "Masked-region min"
column is garment surface inside skin that the mask hides. It is reported only
for information.

influences, normalization error 5e-8. Garments follow the rig (minimum per-piece
RMS motion 5.4 mm).

## Visual check

- **Outfit A:** no skin pokes through. Iteration 1 showed two defects that are
  fixed in the final renders:
  - Dark rims at garment openings, where the eye could see past a hem into the
    hollow masked body. The mask margin fixes this.
  - A skin patch at the crotch of the shorts, a coverage gap in the bottoms
    selector. A crotch bridge in the selector fixes this.
- **Worst pose (sit_down 50):** no poke-through is visible at 640 px. The clip
  frame is a half-squat. The vest bunches at the lap.
- **Dress:** no poke-through. The bodice keeps the first-iteration wide
  armholes, which leave bare shoulders. The short sleeves are separate pieces
  and read as detached cap sleeves. This is a design issue in `_top`, not a
  clearance issue (see remaining issues in the task report).
